# Multiverse Cycle 019 — Thailand When Automation Can Perform Most Work: PRE-REGISTRATION

**Protocol frozen:** 2026-10-20 (Asia/Bangkok), before implementing this run's scenario enumeration.
**Project:** INDEPENDENT Multiverse World Model v4.2.1 (Evidence Gate + bounded qualitative futures). NO AOM or other Skill merger.
**Research question:** If reliable and economical automation eventually becomes capable of performing a very wide range of human tasks, how might life differ across Thai households, and which structural bottlenecks rather than raw capability determine outcomes?
**Status:** HYPOTHETICAL CONDITIONAL EXPLORATION, NOT A DATE-SPECIFIC FORECAST.
**Operational scope:** Public macroeconomic and institutional data only; Test-LaB documents/tests. NO Thai citizen private data, individual profiling, persuasion/party ranking, production mutations or background monitoring.

## 2. State-contract and information gates

S0: 2026 observed Thai economic/demographic starting point, with source date/denominator provenance.
S1: Unknown future date T* at which FULL AUTOMATION CAPABILITY is ASSUMED true. No prediction that T* occurs by 2030, 2040 or at all. Full capability means digital + appropriately robotic systems can perform the vast majority of economically feasible tasks at competitive quality/cost, **conditional on reliability and human acceptance**.
S2: First 0–3 years AFTER T*, with varying adoption costs, ownership, law, demand, and social protection.
S3: 5–10 years AFTER T*, when household balance sheets, market demand, access, and government finance feedback may accumulate.
No future S2/S3 is OBSERVED_REALITY at 2026 cutoff.

Explicit distinction: technical capability != deployment != affordable access != tasks actually replaced != people displaced != income secured.

## 3. Fixed axes — 16 declared hypothetical worlds

4 axes × 2 values each (16 combinations; no numerical probabilities):
- A: AUTOMATION_ACCESS = BROAD_ACCESS | CONCENTRATED_ACCESS
- B: PRODUCTIVITY_GAINS = WIDELY_DISTRIBUTED | HIGHLY_CONCENTRATED
- C: INCOME_PROTECTION = PORTABLE_INCLUSIVE | PATCHY_JOB_TIED
- D: HUMAN_COMPLEMENTARY_DEMAND = ROBUST_TRANSITION | WEAK_TRANSITION

Independence is a branching bookkeeping device ONLY; in reality A/B/C/D may be causally dependent. The model MUST explicitly label that as UNCALIBRATED. No equally likely worlds, no actors/party ranking.

Each world is evaluated against **six synthetic household archetypes** (no real personal data):
(1) informal rural/farm household
(2) formal routine manufacturing worker
(3) urban service/clerical worker
(4) local SME microbusiness owner
(5) older care-needing person/family caregiver
(6) young labor-market entrant.

Write conditional outcome dimensions, NOT a numerical "future happiness index": income-source fragility, access gaps, financial buffer/protection, new-work avenue, access to essential/care services, market concentration, demand feedback and risk of lost entry-level learning pathway. Each flag must be traceable to the declared axes and archetype constraints; source facts only justify why these groups merit consideration, NOT numerical future effects.

## 4. Budgeted scenario exploration

One global, synthetic WORK_UNIT ledger per root. Root expansion costs 1+16 scenario edges = 17; expanding each candidate world and its six group implications costs 1+6 = 7. Complete full grid = 17+16×7 = 129 modeled units. Limited budget 59 expands root +6 worlds, leaving ten world families unexplored. Use transparent maximin-Hamming-diversity ordering so the first two explored worlds differ across all four axes; avoid alphabetical first-world favoritism. Synthetic units are neither inference tokens, CPU, human review hours nor THB.

No probabilities are assigned. Budget exhaustion leaves UNEXPLORED paths, not negative outcomes. Without calibrated labor replacement and real action-dependent causal data, remain QUALITATIVE_SCENARIOS. A complete combinatorial grid is not complete coverage of all Thailand futures.

## 5. Transparent financial identity — toy only

Illustrative monthly household "unit" budget, NOT observed Thai baht or forecast:
Baseline labor income 100 units, essential expense 60, debt service 20 => net 20.
Scenario A: labor income falls 40%, essential goods/services costs fall 20%, fixed debt remains 20 => net -8.
Scenario B: same income/essential/debt assumptions plus hypothetical accessible transfer/dividend 30 => net +22.
This demonstrates the identity net=earned_income+capital_income+transfers-essential_consumption-debt_service; it DOES NOT establish that income will actually fall 40%, prices 20% or any transfer will be paid. No transfers treated as cost-free: fiscal funding, taxing capital gains and trade/price effects are UNKNOWN.
All figures are dimensionless toy inputs, not household surveys.

## 6. Predeclared acceptance gates

1. Public Thai baseline claims with dates/denominators and independent-source status; incomplete source -> UNKNOWN, not fabricated.
2. No 2025 informal/2026 employed denominator mixing; no application of IMF region-level exposure rate to Thailand's 41.51m employed.
3. The FULL capability premise is always flagged ASSUMED not achieved.
4. 2^4=16 distinct worlds, exactly six profiles per expanded world, total 96 group implications when fully explored.
5. No scenario probability, no unemployment rate prediction, no dollar/THB claims, no political actor ranking.
6. Uniform global budget accounting 129 complete; budget 59 -> six worlds expanded and ten unresolved.
7. Diverse exploration: opposite all-four-bit worlds examined first when limited.
8. All scenarios retain possibility that capability exists while effective access is concentrated and purchasing-power shortages persist.
9. Automatic unemployment/full income equality is not inferred from full technical capability.
10. S2/S3 are hypothetical; no simulated child can rewrite 2026 source facts.
11. Micro-household accounting checks 20, -8, +22 and nonlinear "cheap goods alone cannot guarantee affordability".
12. Distinguish personal wages from wealth/dividends/public benefits, and debt service from consumption.
13. No causal or probability certification without independently identified transitions.
14. Profile-level mechanisms are assumption-derived not empirical predicted outcomes.
15. AOM, other Skills and production systems remain unchanged.
16. A README/test/result allow reproduction without new plugin, app or scheduler.

**Outcome to report**: conditional *structural* differences, what sources show as of 2026, what a complete-automation regime could logically entail and what remains UNKNOWN. Re-test on genuinely new data later only by explicit request; no background work.
